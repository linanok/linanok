# Security Policy

If you find a security issue in Linanok, please let us know by emailing **[security@linanok.com](mailto:security@linanok.com)** with details such as how to reproduce the problem, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and any proof-of-concept if possible. We ask that you do not disrupt services or access more data than needed to show the issue. We will confirm your report within 72 hours, review it, and fix it based on its seriousness. If needed, we will work with you on when and how to share the details. Thank you for helping keep Linanok safe.
